About working with Saltwater

Saltwater Business Studio creates custom websites for small businesses. We design and build professional, mobile-friendly websites, along with features such as contact forms, inquiry forms, scheduling, payment integrations, and other tools your business may need. We work with businesses in Sarasota, Venice, and throughout Southwest Florida.

We work with small businesses that need a professional online presence, whether you're starting a business, relying mostly on social media, or ready to replace an outdated website. Our projects can work well for service businesses, independent professionals, salons and beauty businesses, creative businesses, home-service companies, consultants, studios, and other small businesses.

Social media can be an important way for customers to discover you, but it isn't a substitute for having a website you control. Your website gives customers one place to learn about your business, understand your services, see your work, find your contact information, and take the next step.

Why work with Saltwater?

Website builders make it easy to put something online. The harder part is creating a website that is actually easy to navigate, visually distinctive, clear to customers, and structured so search engines can understand your business.

Saltwater handles those decisions for you. We think through the structure of your pages, how customers move through the site, what information they need, how your services are presented, and how the site is built behind the scenes. The result is more than a collection of template sections — it is a website designed around your actual business.

Wix and Squarespace are useful tools for building a website yourself, but a website platform and a professionally designed website are not the same thing.

Saltwater builds each site around your business rather than starting with a template and asking you to fit your business into it. That gives us more control over the visual design, page structure, navigation, content hierarchy, and technical details of the site.

We also build with search visibility in mind, using clear page structure, descriptive titles and metadata, structured content, and other SEO fundamentals that help search engines understand what your business offers. No website can guarantee a particular search position, but the site should be built to give your business a strong technical and content foundation for being found.

A website can look beautiful and still be frustrating to use. If customers cannot quickly figure out what you offer, where to find information, or what to do next, they may leave before contacting you.

We design navigation around the way a real customer moves through your business. Pages are organized intentionally, important information is easy to find, and calls to action are placed where they make sense instead of simply filling space on a template.

That's the idea. Your website should feel like your business, not like a template that happens to contain your logo.

We pay attention to typography, spacing, imagery, color, layout, hierarchy, and the small visual details that make a site feel intentional. The goal is a polished online presence that gives customers a clear sense of who you are and what you offer before they ever contact you.

You're paying for more than someone putting your information on a page. You're paying for the planning, design, development, structure, and attention to detail that go into making the website work as a whole.

A professionally built site should make your business easier to understand, easier to navigate, easier to find, and easier to contact. That's the difference Saltwater is designed to provide.
